Samjha kya?

An Agent Skill that turns any topic or PDF into a swipeable comic flashcard deck. Three levels, a desi cast, lots of examples. Works on phones and laptops.

npx skills add amishah1998/samjha-kya

Then ask your coding agent: /samjha-kya why we procrastinate, or turn guide.pdf into a samjha-kya deck. Source and docs on GitHub.
