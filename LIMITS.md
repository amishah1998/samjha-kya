# Known limits

Found in testing before release. Each one is a known gap, not a hidden one.

- **Quality follows the model.** The skill sets the structure, the rules and the checks; a weaker model still writes weaker stories. The layout check and the build warnings catch layout problems and the obvious content misses, not every factual slip.
- **Takeaway lines.** In testing, agents still dropped a qualifier ("may", "most adults") in about 2 or 3 takeaway lines per long deck. The skill's final fact pass asks for a re-read; review them yourself for health, money and legal decks.
- **Long decks.** A whole subject or a long PDF becomes 150 or more cards. Every chapter stands alone and the beginner level ends with a summary card, but the fixed closing run (myths, notes, quick checks) takes about 40% of each chapter.
- **Small and sideways phones.** Every card fits, but conversation cards shrink their text to fit: to about 76% on a small phone like the iPhone SE, and to 70 to 85% on a phone held sideways.
- **Browsers.** Checked in Chromium on 18 screen sizes. Safari's collapsing toolbar is modelled in the check, not run on a real iPhone. Firefox is not checked.
- **Scanned PDFs.** Pages that are images have no text to read; run OCR first (for example ocrmypdf).
- **Not advice.** Decks are education. Money, health and legal decks carry a caution on every chapter; they are not a substitute for a professional.
